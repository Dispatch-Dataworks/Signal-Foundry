Signal Foundry Games is the game-development division of Dispatch Dataworks LLC—not a separate company. It is a place for intelligent, engaging games, playable experiments, and ideas that are not quite ready to leave the bench.

We make the games we want to play. Some start with curiosity; others start with a real need at home. We like meaningful decisions, systems worth understanding, and the satisfaction of working something out. Learning can be part of that, but fun still matters.

## Meet the Head Nerd

Ben Townsend is our Head Nerd: a software engineer, a radio and TV person, and a dad. His kids and stepkids are part of the story, and family members are often the first people to try things and tell him what works—and what does not.

WordWeave began as a way to help one of his kids with English grades. That mix of family life, practical problem-solving, and making things is a pretty good introduction to the studio.

## Fewer Barriers, More Playing

Browsers can make it easier to get started, including on devices such as Chromebooks. That is an opportunity we value, not a promise that every project works on every device or that every future game will be browser-only.

Our current titles are free games or playable demos. Future projects may be free, paid, or possibly pay-what-you-want. We are not announcing prices or asking for donations here.

## Room for Work in Progress

The games shelf is for playable work. The workshop is for concepts and prototypes. A project's release status and development activity are separate things: a released game can be in maintenance, and an early idea can be actively developed.

We would rather show those distinctions honestly than pretend every sketch is a finished game.
