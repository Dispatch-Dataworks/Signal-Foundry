## Overview

WordWeave is a released, fully playable browser word game. It belongs on the games shelf: something you can play, rather than an idea waiting to become a game.

## Why We Made This

It started at home. Ben wanted to help one of his kids improve their English grades, and making a game offered a different way into the subject.

That is a good example of how Signal Foundry works: begin with something real, make something we want to use, and see where it leads.

## What Makes It Different

WordWeave has an educational origin without being a hypothetical classroom product. It is a real game that grew from a family need, built for the browser to keep getting started straightforward.
